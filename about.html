A TABLE WITH ROOM FOR EVERYONE

Small folds.
Big-hearted food.

Momo Magic began around a kitchen table. Our founder, Tenzin, grew up watching family and friends gather over trays of freshly made momos—each one folded by hand, each meal a reason to stay a little longer.

That same spirit found its home in Little Tibet Lane. Today, we bring together familiar Himalayan comfort and a few playful new favorites, always made fresh and served with a smile.

Come as you are. There’s always room for one more.
